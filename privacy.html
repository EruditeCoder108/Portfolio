Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 19, 2026 • Last Updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how applications, software, games, and digital tools developed and published by Sambhav Jain ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") collect, use, and protect your information.

01

Introduction & Scope

We are committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our users. This policy applies to all mobile applications, web apps, and software published by Sambhav Jain on the Google Play Store, Netlify, and other distribution channels (collectively, the "Services").

By downloading, accessing, or using any of our Services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not install or use our Services.

02

Information We Collect

We adhere to a strict policy of data minimization. The types of data we may collect include:

Device & Technical Information

Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, unique device identifiers, and general system diagnostics to optimize performance and prevent crashes.

Usage & Performance Metrics

Aggregated telemetry such as feature interactions, session durations, and anonymized error stack traces via Google Play Console to ensure app stability.

User-Provided Data

Information you voluntarily provide when contacting developer support (e.g., your name, email address, and feedback message).

03

App Permissions & Hardware Access

Our applications only request runtime permissions that are strictly necessary for core application functionality. Where required:

  • Internet Access: To communicate with APIs, fetch real-time data, and synchronize settings if supported by the app.
  • Camera / Storage / Media: Only requested if the app provides image scanning, media playback, or local file export features (e.g., exporting vCards or document reports). We never access your files without explicit user interaction.
  • Notifications: Used solely to alert you to critical in-app status updates or scheduled alerts if you opt-in.

You can adjust or revoke these permissions at any time via your device's System Settings.

04

Third-Party Services & SDKs

To deliver reliable services and analyze system stability, our apps may integrate trusted third-party SDKs provided by industry leaders. These services operate under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase / Crashlytics: Used for real-time crash reporting and diagnostic data analysis (Firebase Privacy).
  • Google AdMob: If an application contains advertisements, non-personalized or personalized advertising identifiers may be processed in compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.
05

Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families)

We take children's privacy very seriously. Our Services are not designed to knowingly solicit or collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

If we discover that a child under the relevant age has provided personal data, we take immediate steps to delete such data from our records. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

06

Data Security & Retention

We implement robust security measures, including modern transport-layer encryption (HTTPS/TLS) and secure token storage, to safeguard information. We only retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with legal obligations.

07

Your Rights & Data Deletion Requests

Depending on your location (including rights under the GDPR in the European Union and CCPA/CPRA in California), you have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal information we hold about you.
  • Request the correction, modification, or complete deletion of your data.
  • Opt-out of diagnostic telemetry or non-essential data collection.

To submit a data access or deletion request, please email us directly at EruditeSpartan@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
